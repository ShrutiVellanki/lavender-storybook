# How AI was used

I used AI to accelerate the first pass of the system rather than to make the final design decisions.

AI was useful for a one shot generation of most components.

However, several outputs required (and still require) correction.

## Example corrections

1. **Token architecture:** Which tokens are part of the primitive layer, the semantic layer?
2. **Interaction states:** Generated components frequently covered default and hover states but missed combinations such as invalid-and-disabled, loading, keyboard focus, or read-only.
3. **Accessibility**
4. **Responsive behaviour**
5. **Component API:** AI introduced complex component APIs that could be further simplified
